## ⚡ How the 1-Click C3 Gmail Composer Works

Traditional Google SMTP frequently fails with `535-5.7.8 BadCredentials` and Google App Passwords often return *"The setting that you are looking for is not available for your account"*.

To make sending bulletproof and seamless:
1. When you click **`[ ✉ C3 Mail ]`** or **`[ ✉ Send Official C3 Mail ]`**, the system calls:
   ```
   https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=c3.collective.in@gmail.com&view=cm&fs=1&to=<candidate_email>&su=<subject>&body=<body>
   ```
2. Because you are logged into `c3.collective.in@gmail.com` in your web browser, Google automatically opens your official inbox composer.
3. The collegiate admission letter is completely pre-filled with:
   - Candidate recipient email
   - Subject: `🎉 Official Notice of Admission: C3 Batch 01 (Founder Key: XXXX)`
   - Pure 4-character key
   - Direct link to view/print official acceptance letter
   - Direct link to claim 3D pass
   - Workspace venue (Lab 3) and timings (Monday – Thursday, 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM)
   - Official sign-off by Mohammad Bilal (Lead Organizer & Student Convener)
4. Just click **Send** in Gmail! Zero passwords or complex setups needed.
